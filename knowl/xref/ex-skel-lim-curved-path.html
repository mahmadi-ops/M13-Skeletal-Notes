Example 4.2.13. Straight Lines Are Not Enough.

Evaluate the following limit, or show that it does not exist.
\begin{equation*} \lim_{(x,y)\to(1,2)} \frac{(x-1)^2(y-2)^3}{(x-1)^4+(y-2)^6}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Solution. (Shift the point to the origin with \(u = x - 1\text{,}\) \(v = y - 2\text{.}\) Find what every straight line through \((1,2)\) gives, including the vertical one, then try a curve along which the two terms of the denominator are equal, and conclude.)
