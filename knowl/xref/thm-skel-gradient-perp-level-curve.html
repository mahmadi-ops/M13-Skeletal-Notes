Theorem 4.5.10. The Gradient is Perpendicular to the Level Curves.

Let \(f(x,y)\) be a differentiable function, and let \(C\) be the level curve \(f(x,y) = c\) for a constant \(c\text{.}\) At every point of \(C\) where \(\nabla f \neq \mathbf 0\text{,}\) the gradient vector \(\nabla f\) is perpendicular to the level curve \(C\text{.}\)
