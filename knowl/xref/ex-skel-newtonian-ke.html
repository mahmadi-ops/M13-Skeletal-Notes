Example 2.3.21. Newtonian kinetic energy as an approximation to relativistic kinetic energy when \(v \ll c\).

Show that the kinetic energy of an object moving with velocity \(v\) can be approximated by the formula \(K \approx \frac{1}{2} m_0v^2\) when \(v \ll c\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
🔗
When \(v \ll c\text{,}\) we can therefore approximate the kinetic energy as:
\begin{equation} K \approx \frac{1}{2} m_0v^2.\tag{2.3.13} \end{equation}
